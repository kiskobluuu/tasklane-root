Website Privacy & Advertising

This notice explains privacy practices for the public Task Lane Company website and the use of advertising technologies. Job applicants should also review the separate Applicant Privacy Notice.

Information you provide

If you contact Task Lane Company, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message. Information submitted through the careers application is handled as described in the Applicant Privacy Notice.

Advertising and cookies

Task Lane Company may use Google AdSense to display advertising on eligible public pages. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to serve, measure and personalize advertising where permitted. Advertising technologies may also be used to limit repeated ads, measure ad performance and help detect fraud or abuse.

How Google uses information

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services on its How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page. Google's advertising privacy information and available controls are also described in its Advertising documentation.

Your advertising choices

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be offered consent or privacy choices for advertising technologies. You can also review Google's advertising controls through Google's privacy and advertising settings. Browser settings may allow you to delete or restrict cookies, although doing so can affect some website functionality.

Service providers and security

Task Lane may use service providers for website hosting, database services, file storage, email delivery, security, abuse prevention and advertising. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the services we operate, but no internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.
