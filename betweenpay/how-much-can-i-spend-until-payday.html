PAYDAY SPENDING GUIDE

How much can I spend until payday?

Your account balance is only the starting point. A more useful number subtracts what must survive until the next paycheck before you decide what is available for flexible spending.

The simple formula

Current money − bills due before payday − protected goals − safety buffer = estimated Safe to Spend

For example, if you have $1,450 now, need $736 for bills before payday, want to protect a $100 goal and keep a $100 buffer, the amount left is $514. If payday is seven days away, that is roughly $73.43 per day if those protected amounts do not change.

Why your bank balance can be misleading

A balance can look large even when much of it already belongs to rent, utilities, subscriptions, debt payments, savings goals or expenses that arrive before your next paycheck. Treating all of it as spendable can create a shortfall later in the pay cycle.

What to include before calling money “safe to spend”

  • Bills and required payments due before your next payday.
  • Money you deliberately want to protect for savings or a goal.
  • A safety buffer you do not want everyday purchases to use.
  • Any other amount that is already spoken for during this pay period.
