BetweenPay $10,000 Referral Challenge — Official Rules

Rules version: October 4, 2026. Contest status: The contest does not begin until the official contest page displays LIVE NOW with a published start and end time. The live contest period lasts 30 days.

1. Eligibility

Participants must be at least 18 years old, must have successfully purchased BetweenPay, must join using the email associated with their active BetweenPay purchase, must accept these Official Rules, and must be legally eligible to participate and receive a cash prize where they reside. Void where prohibited. People responsible for administering or operating the contest are not eligible to receive a prize.

2. How to enter

An eligible BetweenPay customer joins through the My Referrals dashboard and receives one unique referral link. Joining before the live start time does not create any referral credit before the contest begins.

3. How referrals qualify

A qualified referral is a new BetweenPay customer who reaches BetweenPay through a participant's unique referral link and successfully completes a valid BetweenPay purchase during the published live contest period. A purchaser who already completed a BetweenPay purchase before the referred order is not a new customer for this contest.

Self-referrals, duplicate purchases, multiple access emails controlled by the same purchaser, fraudulent or manipulated transactions, chargebacks, refunded or reversed transactions, transactions outside the contest period, and transactions that cannot be verified are excluded. The same purchaser or PayPal payer may qualify only once for the contest. Each qualified purchaser can be attributed to only one referrer.

4. Ranking and tie-breaker

Participants are ranked by total qualified referrals recorded during the contest period. If two or more participants have the same qualified-referral count, the participant who reached that tied final total first ranks higher. Rankings may change if a previously counted transaction is refunded, reversed, duplicated, or disqualified during verification.

5. Cash prizes

The cash prizes are: 1st $10,000; 2nd $1,000; 3rd $500; 4th $250; 5th $100; 6th $60; 7th $50; 8th $40; 9th $30; and 10th $20. Total advertised prize pool: $12,050 USD. Prizes are subject to winner verification and any taxes or reporting obligations applicable to the winner.

6. Winner verification

Final standings are subject to verification of the participant's qualifying BetweenPay purchase, referral attribution, referred purchases, identity, age, eligibility, and payment status. Potential winners may be asked for reasonable information necessary to confirm eligibility and arrange prize payment. Fraud, transaction manipulation, artificial purchases, or other violations of these rules may result in disqualification and removal of associated referrals.

7. Refunds, reversals and disputes

A purchase that is refunded, reversed, charged back, or otherwise invalidated does not count as a qualified referral. If such a transaction was previously reflected in the leaderboard, it may be removed and rankings recalculated.

8. Testing and demo accounts

BetweenPay may use clearly designated internal demo or testing accounts to test ranking calculations while the contest is live. Demo/testing accounts are excluded from the official public leaderboard, cannot win prizes, and cannot reduce or replace a real participant's rank or prize eligibility.

9. Technical integrity

BetweenPay may correct technical errors, remove invalid transactions, temporarily pause affected functionality, or recalculate standings when necessary to preserve accurate contest operation. Any material change affecting the contest period, prizes, or participant eligibility will be published on the official contest page.

10. Platform independence

The BetweenPay Referral Challenge is operated by BetweenPay/Task Lane and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by PayPal or any social-media platform used to share referral links.
