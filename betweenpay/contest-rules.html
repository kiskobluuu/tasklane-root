OFFICIAL CONTEST RULES

BetweenPay $10,000 Referral Challenge

These rules govern eligibility, referral qualification, rankings, cash prizes, winner verification and contest integrity.

Contest length30 days from the published LIVE NOW start time.
Top prize$10,000 USD cash for verified 1st place.
Total prize pool$12,050 USD across the verified Top 10.

Rules version: October 5, 2026. Contest status: The contest does not begin until the official contest page displays LIVE NOW with a published start and end time. The live contest period lasts 30 days.

1. Eligibility

Participants must be at least 18 years old, must have successfully purchased BetweenPay, must join using the email associated with their active BetweenPay purchase, must accept these Official Rules, and must be legally eligible to participate and receive a cash prize where they reside. Void where prohibited. People responsible for administering or operating the contest are not eligible to receive a prize.

2. How to enter

An eligible BetweenPay customer joins through the My Referrals dashboard, selects a public leaderboard display name, accepts these Official Rules, and receives one unique referral link. Joining before the live start time does not create referral credit before the contest begins.

3. How referrals qualify

A qualified referral is a new BetweenPay customer who reaches BetweenPay through a participant's unique referral link and successfully completes a valid BetweenPay purchase during the published live contest period. A purchaser who already completed a BetweenPay purchase before the referred order is not a new customer for this contest.

Self-referrals, duplicate purchases, multiple access emails controlled by the same purchaser, fraudulent or manipulated transactions, chargebacks, refunded or reversed transactions, transactions outside the contest period, and transactions that cannot be verified are excluded. The same purchaser or PayPal payer may qualify only once for the contest. Each qualified purchaser can be attributed to only one referrer.

4. Referral sharing and disclosure

When promoting or sharing a BetweenPay referral link, participants must clearly disclose that they are participating in a cash-prize referral challenge and may benefit if a person purchases through their referral link. The disclosure should be easy to notice and included with the promotional message itself. Participants must not make false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about BetweenPay, the contest, prizes, or their likelihood of winning.

5. Ranking and tie-breaker

Participants are ranked by total qualified referrals recorded during the contest period. If two or more participants have the same qualified-referral count, the participant who reached that tied final total first ranks higher. Rankings may change if a previously counted transaction is refunded, reversed, duplicated, or disqualified during verification.

6. Cash prizes

The cash prizes are: 1st $10,000; 2nd $1,000; 3rd $500; 4th $250; 5th $100; 6th $60; 7th $50; 8th $40; 9th $30; and 10th $20. Total advertised prize pool: $12,050 USD.

Prizes are subject to winner verification and any taxes, reporting requirements, payment restrictions, or legal obligations applicable to the winner.

7. Winner verification

Final standings are subject to verification of the participant's qualifying BetweenPay purchase, referral attribution, referred purchases, identity, age, eligibility, and payment status. Potential winners may be asked for reasonable information necessary to confirm eligibility and arrange prize payment.

Fraud, transaction manipulation, artificial purchases, false identities, coordinated duplicate purchasing, or other violations of these rules may result in disqualification and removal of associated referrals.

8. Refunds, reversals and disputes

A purchase that is refunded, reversed, charged back, or otherwise invalidated does not count as a qualified referral. If such a transaction was previously reflected in the leaderboard, it may be removed and rankings recalculated.

9. Technical integrity and corrections

BetweenPay may correct technical errors, remove invalid transactions, temporarily pause affected functionality, review suspicious activity, or recalculate standings when reasonably necessary to preserve accurate and fair contest operation. Any material change affecting the contest period, advertised prizes, or participant eligibility will be published on the official contest page.

10. Winner contact and prize payment

Potential winners may be contacted using the email associated with their BetweenPay contest entry. A potential winner must respond to reasonable verification or payout requests within the stated timeframe provided in that communication. Failure to complete required verification, ineligibility, or a rules violation may result in disqualification and adjustment of final placements.

11. Platform independence

The BetweenPay Referral Challenge is operated by BetweenPay / Task Lane and is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with PayPal or any social-media platform used to share referral links.

12. Governing availability

The contest is offered only where lawful. Participants are responsible for confirming that entering and receiving a cash prize is permitted where they reside. BetweenPay may decline or revoke participation where required by law, payment restrictions, sanctions, compliance requirements, or these Official Rules.
