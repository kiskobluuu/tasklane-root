BetweenPay $10,000 Referral Challenge — Official Rules

Contest status: The contest does not begin until the published start date appears on the official leaderboard. The live contest runs for 30 days.

Eligibility

Participants must be at least 18 years old, must have successfully purchased BetweenPay, must join the referral challenge using the email associated with their BetweenPay purchase, and must be legally eligible to participate and receive a cash prize where they reside. Void where prohibited.

How referrals qualify

A qualified referral is a new BetweenPay customer who follows a participant's unique referral link and successfully completes a valid BetweenPay purchase during the live contest period. Self-referrals, duplicate purchases, fraudulent transactions, chargebacks, refunded or reversed transactions, and transactions that cannot be verified are excluded. Each new purchaser can qualify for only one referrer.

Ranking

Participants are ranked by total qualified referrals during the contest period. If two participants finish with the same qualified-referral count, the participant who reached that final count first ranks higher. Official rankings exclude demo and testing accounts.

Prizes

The cash prizes are: 1st $10,000; 2nd $1,000; 3rd $500; 4th $250; 5th $100; 6th $60; 7th $50; 8th $40; 9th $30; 10th $20. Total advertised prize pool: $12,050 USD.

Winner verification

Final standings are subject to verification of purchases, referral attribution, participant identity and eligibility. Potential winners may be required to provide reasonable information necessary to verify eligibility and arrange payment. A participant who used fraud, manipulated transactions or otherwise violated these rules may be disqualified.

Testing and demo accounts

BetweenPay may use clearly designated internal demo or testing accounts to test leaderboard calculations while the contest is live. Demo/testing accounts are excluded from official public rankings, cannot win prizes, and do not reduce or replace a real participant's ranking or prize eligibility.

Changes and technical issues

BetweenPay may correct technical errors, remove invalid transactions, or pause the contest if a material technical or security issue affects fair operation. Any material change affecting participants will be published on the contest page.
