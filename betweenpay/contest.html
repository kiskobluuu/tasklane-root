QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Who can enter?

Eligible BetweenPay customers who are at least 18, have a valid active purchase, accept the Official Rules, and may legally participate where they live.

What counts as a qualified referral?

A new customer attributed to your unique referral link who completes a valid BetweenPay purchase during the live contest period and passes verification.

When does the contest begin?

Only when this official page displays LIVE NOW with published start and end timestamps. The live period lasts 30 days.

How are ties handled?

If participants finish with the same qualified-referral total, the participant who reached that tied total first ranks higher.

Do refunds still count?

No. Refunded, reversed, charged-back, duplicate, self-referred, fraudulent, or otherwise invalid transactions do not qualify and may be removed from standings.

When are winners paid?

Final standings are verified after the contest closes. Potential winners must complete any reasonable eligibility and payment verification required before prizes are released.
