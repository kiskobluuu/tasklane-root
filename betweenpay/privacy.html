Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

BetweenPay uses the information needed to provide purchase access, secure sign-in, and synchronization of your budgeting plan across signed-in devices.

Purchase and account information

We store the email associated with your purchase, product-access status, and payment identifiers needed to confirm or revoke access. PayPal processes payment credentials; BetweenPay does not receive your PayPal password or full card details.

Your budgeting data

When you use BetweenPay while signed in, your budgeting plan, including your balance, paychecks, bills, goals, profile settings and safety buffer, is saved to your BetweenPay account through Supabase so your plan can be restored when you sign in on another supported browser or device.

Browser storage and cloud synchronization

BetweenPay also keeps an account-specific copy of your plan in browser storage on the device you are using. The signed-in cloud copy is used to synchronize and restore planner data across devices. Clearing browser or site data may remove the local copy from that device, but data already synchronized to your account can be restored after you sign in again.

Service providers

We use PayPal for payment processing and Supabase for authentication, purchase-access records and synchronized account data. Those services process information under their own applicable privacy terms.
