Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

BetweenPay uses the information needed to provide purchase access, secure sign-in, and synchronization of your budgeting plan across signed-in devices.

Purchase and account information

We store the email associated with your purchase, product-access status, and payment identifiers needed to confirm or revoke access. PayPal processes payment credentials; BetweenPay does not receive your PayPal password or full card details.

Your budgeting data

When you use BetweenPay while signed in, your budgeting plan, including your balance, paychecks, bills, goals, profile settings and safety buffer, is saved to your BetweenPay account through Supabase so your plan can be restored when you sign in on another supported browser or device.

Browser storage and cloud synchronization

BetweenPay also keeps an account-specific copy of your plan in browser storage on the device you are using. The signed-in cloud copy is used to synchronize and restore planner data across devices. Clearing browser or site data may remove the local copy from that device, but data already synchronized to your account can be restored after you sign in again.

Marketing emails and checkout reminders

If you choose to opt in, BetweenPay may send the free payday checklist, product tips and limited checkout reminders. We store your email-marketing consent, campaign/source information used for attribution, email-delivery status and unsubscribe status. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in marketing emails. Transactional purchase, account-access and support emails are separate from marketing emails.

Support chat and support requests

If you use the BetweenPay Support Assistant or submit a support request, we store the conversation transcript and the information you provide, such as your email address, question, support category and the page where the chat began. Chat transcripts are used to provide customer support and are sent to the BetweenPay Support inbox. Support-chat and support-form records are stored through Supabase, and transactional support notifications may be delivered through our email service provider.

The Support Assistant is automated and is not a live human agent. If its answer is not sufficient, you can submit a support request for an email reply.

Service providers

We use PayPal for payment processing and Supabase for authentication, purchase-access records, synchronized account data and support records. We also use an email delivery provider for transactional account and support messages. Those services process information under their own applicable privacy terms.
